CRM Developer

Muhammad Afzal Kalwar is a CRM developer in Islamabad, Pakistan. He is available remote for US/UK/EU teams that need lead intake, campaign tools, or an admin dashboard around an existing process.

Fiverr Lead Extractor CRM combines Playwright workers, MongoDB, BullMQ queues, Excel export, and an Electron package. SMS Marketing CRM is a consent-aware campaign dashboard with Vonage. The email verification platform stores bulk-check history. Indus Carrier Directory is a searchable carrier UI fed by SAFER data.

He will scope a CRM to the workflow you already have. The public apps above are the examples, not a promise of unnamed customers.
