FAQ

How does bulk email verification work?

My verifier checks syntax, looks up the domain's MX records and runs an SMTP mailbox check. Some mail servers don't give a definitive answer, so results are never 100% certain.

Can it run on my own server?

Yes. bulk-email-verifier is self-hosted and Docker-ready, so the list stays on your infrastructure.

Can it read my existing CSV or Excel export?

It finds email addresses in any column of a CSV or XLSX file and exports a valid-only list with your original columns.

Do you build cold email sending tools?

I have published multi-SMTP sending automation. Any outreach tool should be used in line with CAN-SPAM, GDPR/PECR and your mail provider's rules.
