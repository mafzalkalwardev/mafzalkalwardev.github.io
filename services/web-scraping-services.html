Problems this solves

Copying data from websites into spreadsheets by hand

Carrier or lead lists that go stale because nobody re-checks them

One-off scripts that break and nobody can re-run

Data scattered across PDFs and web pages

FAQ

What kind of websites can you scrape?

Public pages that the site's terms and the law allow. Feasibility depends on the site, so I check it before committing to anything.

Can you extract FMCSA / SAFER carrier data?

Yes, I have public projects for it (fmcsa-safer-scraper, safer-carrier-extractor, indus-carrier-directory). Where FMCSA offers official datasets or APIs, those are usually the better source.

What formats do you deliver data in?

My existing tools export CSV and Excel and store data in MongoDB. Other formats can be agreed during scoping.

Do you maintain scrapers when sites change?

Sites do change. Whether ongoing maintenance is included is part of the scope we would agree in writing.

How much does a custom scraper cost?

Pricing depends on scope - contact me with what you need and I'll reply with questions before any estimate.
